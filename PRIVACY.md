# Privacy policy

Updated October 3, 2026 · UCE Evidence Lens for Gemini public preview

## Operator and scope

5 Race Street LLC, operating under the registered alternate names Copyright by
UCE and CbyUCE, provides the skill, release documentation, and remote UCE
connector. Contact [support@universalcreationevidence.com](mailto:support@universalcreationevidence.com)
with privacy questions or requests.

## Information processed

When Gemini calls a UCE tool, the service receives the public record reference
in that call. It retrieves the corresponding approved public record and
chronology sources and returns checks, source links, timestamps, verifier
provenance, coverage limits, and recorded public assertions. Assertions can
include names and statements about identity, authorship, ownership, rights,
consent, or creation dates. They remain untrusted public record text.

The tools accept public references, not original works, private manifests,
accounts, payment information, or credentials. Do not send sensitive information.
The connector does not request your full Gemini conversation. What Gemini sends
to a connected app is controlled by Google and your usage of its features.

The hosting layer may process ordinary network and protocol metadata, including
request time, route, status, user agent, and network address. The application does
not intentionally log request bodies, record references, inspection results, or
conversation text.

## Storage and providers

Inspection data is processed in memory for the request. The application has no
inspection database, persistent report store, UCE user accounts, cookies, or
application analytics. Results are returned to Gemini. Saving or sharing them
is your choice. Operational, security, and provider records can be retained under
the relevant provider's policies; these application choices do not mean that
every provider keeps no data.

- **Google and Gemini:** your conversation, tool calls, results, tasks, and
  activity follow Google's policies and your account controls. Review the
  [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961).
- **Google Cloud:** the shared connector runs on Cloud Run in `us-central1`.
  Hosting follows the [Google Cloud Privacy Notice](https://cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice).
- **GitHub:** hosts the public release files and documentation. Requests to those
  pages and downloads follow the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Public sources:** CbyUCE, Arweave, and Turbo Gateway receive bounded retrieval
  requests needed for a record's supported checks and apply their own policies.

The downloadable skill contains plain-text instructions and references; it does
not execute a background agent or start a local server. The release website
contains no analytics, forms, cookies, or third-party scripts.

## Your choices

You can disconnect the custom app, deactivate or remove the skill, and manage
conversation/activity retention through Google. You may request correction or
deletion of support correspondence we control, subject to operational and legal
obligations. We cannot delete public blockchain records or data controlled by
Google, GitHub, public gateways, or other providers.

Support messages contain the information you choose to send. Please omit private
works, credentials, and unrelated personal information. We use correspondence
to respond and resolve issues and retain it as needed for that purpose and our
legal or security obligations.

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above identifies the
current version.
